Exits use the scanner SMC structural stop and opposing-liquidity target (not a fixed R multiple). Structural stop limits: NIFTY up to 60 points; BANKNIFTY and SENSEX up to 150 points. Target distance must exceed stop distance. If the first completed trade of the day is profitable, further entries are blocked for that day.
